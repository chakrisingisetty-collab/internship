SnapBook – 5‑Colour UI Palette

#	Colour name	HEX	UI purpose	Recommended usage
1	Midnight Navy	#1A2B4C	Primary	Header bar, top navigation, primary CTA background (e.g., “Book Now”). Works well on both light and dark themes; white or light‑gray text meets WCAG AA contrast.
2	Soft Slate	#F5F7FA	Background	Main screen background, card surfaces, modal backdrops. Light enough to keep photography images dominant while providing a clean, premium feel.
3	Royal Gold	#D4AF37	Accent	Highlight icons, active filter chips, price tags, and subtle hover/focus outlines. Adds a creative, premium sparkle without overwhelming the layout.
4	Cool Charcoal	#4E5A66	Secondary / Text	Body copy, secondary headings, sub‑labels, and disabled states. Dark enough for high readability on the Soft Slate background; pairs nicely with Midnight Navy for hierarchy.
5	Fresh Aqua	#00A8A8	Interactive / Feedback	Success messages, “Add to Favorites” heart, link states, and onboarding illustrations. Provides a friendly, trustworthy pop of colour that stands out against both dark and light surfaces.
Why this palette works for SnapBook

Trustworthy & professional: Midnight Navy and Cool Charcoal convey stability and expertise.
Creative & premium: Royal Gold adds a luxe accent that feels artistic without being flashy.
Friendly: Fresh Aqua introduces a welcoming, approachable tone for interactive moments.
Balanced contrast: Each text/interactive colour meets WCAG AA contrast ratios against the Soft Slate background, ensuring accessibility on both light and dark modes.
