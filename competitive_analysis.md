# SnapBook — Competitive UX Analysis

## 1. Objective

To analyse and compare leading photography booking platforms to identify strengths, gaps, and opportunities, and to derive insights that can help improve the user experience of SnapBook.

## 2. Competitors

### Urban Company
Multi-category home services, including event photography.

### WedMeGood
Wedding planning platform with photographers and wedding vendors.

### ShaadiSaga
Wedding-focused platform for photographers and wedding services.

## 3. UX Evaluation Criteria

1. Ease of Navigation
2. Search & Discovery
3. Filtering
4. Photographer Portfolio
5. Pricing Transparency
6. Availability Information
7. Booking Flow
8. Reviews & Ratings
9. Visual Design
10. Overall Usability

## 4. Key Findings

- Urban Company offers the most seamless booking experience.
- WedMeGood has strong discovery and vendor variety for weddings.
- ShaadiSaga provides rich visual content but lacks a smooth booking experience.
- All three competitors have gaps in personalisation, real-time availability, and inspiration-based discovery.

## 7. UX Opportunities for SnapBook

### 1. Real-time Availability + Instant Booking
Offer live availability and instant booking confirmation for photographers.

### 2. Personalised Photographer Matching
Use user preferences, event type, and style to recommend the most relevant photographers.

### 3. Inspiration-led Discovery
Provide curated collections, mood boards, and real event galleries to help users find ideas and photographers more easily.

## 8. 1-Page Competitive Brief

### Market Context
The photography booking market in India is growing, with platforms like Urban Company, WedMeGood, and ShaadiSaga catering to different user needs. While these platforms offer strong features, there are opportunities to create a more personalised, transparent, and seamless experience.

### Key Takeaways
- Urban Company leads in usability and booking flow.
- WedMeGood excels in discovery and variety.
- ShaadiSaga offers strong visual content.
- Common gaps include real-time availability, personalisation, and inspiration-led discovery.

### Opportunity for SnapBook
SnapBook can differentiate by combining seamless booking, personalised recommendations, and inspiring visual discovery — creating a more engaging and trustworthy experience for users looking to capture their special moments.

## 9. Conclusion

Competitor analysis shows that while existing platforms offer valuable features, there is clear room for innovation. By addressing key gaps and focusing on user-centric design, SnapBook can deliver a superior photography booking experience in India.
