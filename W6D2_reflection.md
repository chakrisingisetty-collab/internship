# W6D2 — Reflection

Conducting user interview research helped me understand how users evaluate photographers before booking. The findings showed that trust, pricing clarity, package information, and availability are important factors in the booking process. Creating an affinity map helped me organize the findings into three clear themes. The HMW statements helped convert user problems into design opportunities. The most important opportunity is making photographer packages and prices easier to compare. This research will help guide the next design iteration of the SnapBook booking experience and make the process simpler and more user-friendly.

## Key Concept

Affinity mapping helps organize qualitative research findings into meaningful groups and themes.
