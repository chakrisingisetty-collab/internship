# W7D3 Reflection — WCAG Accessibility Validation

Accessibility validation is important for Indian organisations because digital products must serve users with different visual abilities and devices. For example, a photography booking platform like SnapBook should make photographer names, prices, buttons, and booking statuses easy to understand. WCAG contrast checks help designers identify text and interface colours that may be difficult to see. AI can suggest accessible colour combinations and layouts, but designers must verify contrast ratios and test designs with users. Organisations such as banks, e-commerce companies, and service platforms can use accessibility checks to improve usability and create more inclusive digital experiences. Accessibility should be considered from the beginning of design.
