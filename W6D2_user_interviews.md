# W6D2 — User Interviews

## Project
SnapBook — Photography Booking & Management App

## Feature
Photographer Booking

## Participants
2 Users

---

## Interview Questions

1. How do you currently choose a photographer for an event?
2. What information do you need before booking a photographer?
3. What problems do you face while comparing photographers?
4. What would make the booking process easier for you?
5. What would make you trust a photographer before booking?

---

## Interview Findings

### Participant 1 — Rahul

**Event Type:** Wedding

**Q1. How do you currently choose a photographer for an event?**  
I usually check the photographer's portfolio, reviews, and previous wedding work before choosing.

**Q2. What information do you need before booking a photographer?**  
I need the package price, services included, availability, and delivery details.

**Q3. What problems do you face while comparing photographers?**  
It is difficult to compare different photographers because packages and prices are presented differently.

**Q4. What would make the booking process easier for you?**  
A simple comparison of packages, prices, availability, and services would make booking easier.

**Q5. What would make you trust a photographer before booking?**  
Verified reviews, previous work, and clear photographer information would make me trust them.

**Key Observation:**  
Needs clear package information and trust signals before booking.

---

### Participant 2 — Priya

**Event Type:** Birthday

**Q1. How do you currently choose a photographer for an event?**  
I usually ask friends for recommendations and then check the photographer's social media or portfolio.

**Q2. What information do you need before booking a photographer?**  
I want to know the price, available date, package details, and number of edited photos.

**Q3. What problems do you face while comparing photographers?**  
Comparing prices and services between photographers takes a lot of time.

**Q4. What would make the booking process easier for you?**  
Showing availability and package details in one place would make the process easier.

**Q5. What would make you trust a photographer before booking?**  
Good reviews, previous work, and recommendations make me feel comfortable booking.

**Key Observation:**  
Wants faster comparison with availability, pricing, and trust information in one place.

---

## Synthesis & Key Themes

### 1. Trust & Credibility
- Reviews influence photographer selection.
- Previous work helps users build confidence.
- Recommendations and verified information increase trust.

### 2. Pricing & Package Information
- Users need clear package prices.
- Users want to know included services.
- Comparing different packages is difficult.

### 3. Booking Experience
- Users want clear availability information.
- Comparing photographers takes time.
- Users prefer booking information in one place.

---

## HMW Statements

1. How might we help users quickly find trusted photographers?
2. How might we make photographer packages and prices easier to compare?
3. How might we show photographer availability clearly?
4. How might we make the booking process simple and fast?
5. How might we help users feel confident before confirming a booking?

### Priority HMW

**How might we make photographer packages and prices easier to compare?**

**Reason:**  
Comparing photographers, prices, and services takes too much time.

---

## Outcome

The research identified trust, pricing and package clarity, and booking experience as the main user needs. The priority HMW focuses on making photographer packages and prices easier to compare.
