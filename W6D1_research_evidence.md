# W6D1 — Research Evidence

## Research Method

A short user interview was planned around the SnapBook photographer booking feature.

## Participants

- User 1 — Rahul
- User 2 — Priya

## Key Findings

### Trust & Credibility
Users look for reviews, previous work, recommendations, and verified photographers before booking.

### Pricing & Information
Users want clear package prices, included services, and photographer availability.

### Booking Experience
Users find comparing photographers time-consuming and prefer a simple booking and confirmation process.

## Affinity Map Themes

1. Trust & Credibility
2. Pricing & Information
3. Booking Experience

## HMW Statements

1. How might we help users quickly find trusted photographers?
2. How might we make photographer packages and prices easier to compare?
3. How might we show photographer availability clearly?
4. How might we make the booking process simple and fast?
5. How might we help users feel confident before confirming a booking?

## Priority HMW

How might we make photographer packages and prices easier to compare?

## Design Opportunity

Improve package comparison by presenting photographer pricing, included services, availability, and relevant trust information clearly so users can make faster booking decisions.
