\# SnapBook — How Might We Statements



\## HMW 1 — Photographer Discovery



How might we help users find the right photographer quickly based on event type, location, budget, and style?



\## HMW 2 — Easy Booking



How might we make the photography booking process simple and clear so users can complete a booking with minimal effort?



\## HMW 3 — Booking Confidence



How might we give users enough information about photographers, packages, reviews, availability, and pricing to make a confident booking decision?
