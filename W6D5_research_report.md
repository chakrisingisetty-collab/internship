# W6D5 — Research Report

## Project
SnapBook — Photographer Booking

## 1. Research Objective
Understand how users choose photographers, evaluate trust, compare packages and prices, and complete the booking process.

## 2. Research Method
- Method: Semi-structured user interviews
- Interview questions: 5
- Planned participants: 2
- Status: Previous findings are practice/mock responses. Conduct actual interviews before reporting validated findings.

## 3. Interview Questions
1. How do you currently choose a photographer for an event?
2. What information do you need before booking a photographer?
3. What problems do you face while comparing photographers?
4. What would make the booking process easier for you?
5. What would make you trust a photographer before booking?

## 4. Affinity Mapping — Three Themes

### Theme 1: Trust and Credibility
- Users may check photographer portfolios and reviews.
- Previous work can help users evaluate quality.
- Recommendations and verified information may build trust.

**Insight to validate:** Users need reliable evidence of a photographer's quality before booking.

### Theme 2: Pricing and Package Information
- Users may want clear package prices.
- Users need to understand included services.
- Comparing packages may take time.

**Insight to validate:** Users need an easy way to compare package prices and included services.

### Theme 3: Booking Experience
- Users may want to check photographer availability.
- Comparing photographers may take time.
- Users may prefer booking information in one place.

**Insight to validate:** Users need a simpler booking process with availability and essential details in one place.

## 5. How Might We (HMW) Statements
1. How might we help users quickly find trusted photographers?
2. How might we make photographer packages and prices easier to compare?
3. How might we show photographer availability clearly?
4. How might we make the booking process simple and fast?
5. How might we help users feel confident before confirming a booking?

## 6. Priority Insight
Help users compare photographer packages, prices, included services, availability, and reviews in one clear view.

## 7. Design Opportunity
Create a photographer comparison section in SnapBook with consistent package details, transparent pricing, availability, and reviews.

## 8. Next Steps
- Conduct and document two actual user interviews.
- Validate or revise the three affinity themes.
- Prioritise the HMW statement using evidence from participants.
- Conduct usability tests using the W6D4 test plan.
- Record actual task completion, errors, time taken, and participant feedback.

## 9. Research Limitations
The themes and insights above are hypotheses based on prior practice work. They must be validated with real participant data before being presented as research findings.
